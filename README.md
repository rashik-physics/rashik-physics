## About Me

I am a Lecturer in Physics at the World University of Bangladesh. I hold BSc and thesis-based MSc degrees in Physics from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh.

My current research activities at the University of Dhaka focus on **video-based, non-contact physiological monitoring**. I use Python and OpenCV for region-of-interest (ROI) processing and illumination analysis of video recordings, with an interest in understanding how recording conditions influence physiological signal extraction.

I am interested in pursuing a research PhD where my background in physics can contribute to biomedical imaging, optical sensing, computer vision, or computational research.
