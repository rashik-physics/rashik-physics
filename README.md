## About Me

I am a Lecturer in Physics at the World University of Bangladesh. I hold BSc and thesis-based MSc degrees in Physics from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh.

My current research activities at the University of Dhaka focus on **video-based, non-contact physiological monitoring**. I use Python and OpenCV for region-of-interest (ROI) processing and illumination analysis of video recordings, with an interest in understanding how recording conditions influence physiological signal extraction.

I use Python to explore problems in imaging and computational physics. My portfolio includes a synthetic-video illumination analysis demonstration, and I am developing an educational one-dimensional photonic-crystal calculation using plane-wave expansion.

I am interested in pursuing a research PhD in biomedical imaging, optical sensing, photonics, computer vision, or computational physics.

## Public Code Demonstrations

### [OpenCV Video Illumination Analysis](https://github.com/rashik-physics/opencv-video-analysis-demo)

An educational Python demonstration that generates a synthetic video, analyzes mean Y-channel values within a fixed rectangular region of interest, and exports a time series and plot. It illustrates frame-by-frame processing, ROI selection, and visualization. It does not estimate respiratory rate or provide a validated physiological measurement.

### Computational photonics — educational project

A Python notebook explores the band structure of an infinite one-dimensional dielectric stack using plane-wave expansion. It includes analytic Fourier coefficients, FFT comparisons, a uniform-medium check, and a first-gap convergence study. The prepared notebook's 10 code cells passed their existing assertions in a local verification run. Repository publication is pending.

The model assumes normal incidence and lossless, nondispersive materials. This is a learning project in classical electromagnetism; it does not claim COMSOL validation, experimental results, or a publication.
